Abbreviations used below. MEMS is micro-electro-mechanical systems. LCoS is liquid crystal on silicon. SOA is semiconductor optical amplifier. TRL is technology readiness level. ML is machine learning.

Quotes here are folded to plain ASCII, so the micro sign reads u and the times sign reads x. The CSV keeps every quote verbatim.
