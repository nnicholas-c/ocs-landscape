# Reading list

Ten core papers whose full text would fill the most matrix cells that abstracts left empty, or settle the widest ranges. Picked by the stage 6 analyst. Title and year come from data/db/papers.sqlite, and the cell lists come from deliverables/comparison_matrix.csv. Written by pipeline.matrix_render.

1. W4292950821, "Mission Apollo: Landing Optical Circuit Switching at Datacenter Scale" (2022). Google's production Apollo system paper. A hyperscaler system paper states the design targets for port count, switching time, and optical performance, so it is the best single read for the 3D MEMS gaps and for AI cluster fit. Would fill mems_3d polarization_dependent_loss, mems_3d crosstalk.
2. W2914521323, "A Comparative Review of MEMS-Based Optical Cross-Connects for All-Optical Networks From the Past to the Present Day" (2019). Survey of MEMS cross-connects covering insertion loss, crosstalk, switching speed, and scalability for both 2D and 3D MEMS, where 2D MEMS abstracts are nearly empty. Would fill mems_2d insertion_loss, mems_2d polarization_dependent_loss, mems_2d crosstalk, mems_2d wavelength_range, mems_2d cost_per_port, mems_2d scaling_limit. Would check mems_2d switching_time.
3. W2896156020, "Silicon Photonic Switch Fabrics: Technology and Architecture" (2018). Survey of silicon photonic switch fabrics that defines common metrics across thermo-optic, electro-optic, and MEMS actuation, so one read fills gaps in three routes. Would fill electro_optic polarization_dependent_loss, electro_optic cost_per_port.
4. W2909208749, "State of the Art and Perspectives on Silicon Photonic Switches" (2019). Review of silicon photonic switches with insertion loss, crosstalk, switching time, and packaging per switch engine, the main source for the silicon photonic MEMS cost gap and a cross-check of its wide ranges. Would fill mems_silicon_photonic cost_per_port.
5. W2073240697, "Fiber-handling robot and optical connection mechanisms for automatic cross-connection of multiple optical connectors" (2013). The only core paper on robotic patch panels, so every performance cell for that route depends on its full text. Would fill robotic_patch_panel switching_time, robotic_patch_panel insertion_loss, robotic_patch_panel port_count, robotic_patch_panel polarization_dependent_loss, robotic_patch_panel crosstalk, robotic_patch_panel wavelength_range, robotic_patch_panel cost_per_port, robotic_patch_panel scaling_limit. Would check robotic_patch_panel ai_cluster_fit.
6. W3034334810, "Principle, Design, and Prototyping of Core Selective Switch Using Free-Space Optics for Spatial Channel Network" (2020). LCoS core selective switch prototype. Its full text should give crosstalk, polarization dependent loss, and wavelength range, which the LCoS abstracts leave empty. Would fill lcos polarization_dependent_loss, lcos crosstalk, lcos wavelength_range, lcos scaling_limit. Would check lcos insertion_loss.
7. W2913395450, "Design and Analysis of Beam Steering Multicore Fiber Optical Switches" (2019). Design paper for beam steering multicore switches, the best available source for piezo polarization dependent loss, cost, and switching time. Would fill piezo polarization_dependent_loss, piezo cost_per_port. Would check piezo port_count, piezo crosstalk, piezo wavelength_range.
8. W2002555923, "A Multiport Microsecond Optical Circuit Switch for Data Center Networking" (2013). Mordia datacenter switch. Its full text settles whether its wavelength selective switch is LCoS, which decides if the only LCoS switching time belongs to that route. Fills no empty cell by name. Would check lcos switching_time.
9. W3215039088, "Core Selective Switch With Low Insertion Loss Over Ultra-Wide Wavelength Range for Spatial Channel Networks" (2021). MEMS mirror core selective switch whose stored abstract lost its polarization dependent loss figure to broken math markup. Would fill mems_3d polarization_dependent_loss. Would check mems_3d wavelength_range.
10. W2028213396, "Monolithically Integrated 8:1 SOA Gate Switch With Large Extinction Ratio and Wide Input Power Dynamic Range" (2009). SOA gate switch that claims polarization insensitivity and high extinction without the matching crosstalk and polarization figures. Would fill soa polarization_dependent_loss, soa crosstalk.
