# Reading list

Ten core papers whose full text would fill the most matrix cells that abstracts left empty, or settle the widest ranges. Picked by the stage 6 analyst. Title and year come from data/db/papers.sqlite, and the cell lists come from deliverables/comparison_matrix.csv. Written by pipeline.matrix_render.

MEMS is micro-electro-mechanical systems, LCoS is liquid crystal on silicon, and SOA is semiconductor optical amplifier.

1. W2073240697, "Fiber-handling robot and optical connection mechanisms for automatic cross-connection of multiple optical connectors" (2013). The only core paper on robotic patch panels, so its full text is the one source for nearly every empty cell in that row. Would fill robotic_patch_panel switching_time, robotic_patch_panel insertion_loss, robotic_patch_panel port_count, robotic_patch_panel polarization_dependent_loss, robotic_patch_panel crosstalk, robotic_patch_panel wavelength_range, robotic_patch_panel cost_per_port, robotic_patch_panel scaling_limit.
2. W4292950821, "Mission Apollo: Landing Optical Circuit Switching at Datacenter Scale" (2022). The Google Apollo paper, the one production deployment in the core set, whose full text should give port count, loss, switching time and crosstalk for a shipping three-dimensional MEMS switch. Would fill mems_3d crosstalk.
3. W2914521323, "A Comparative Review of MEMS-Based Optical Cross-Connects for All-Optical Networks From the Past to the Present Day" (2019). A survey of MEMS cross-connects that compares loss, crosstalk, switching speed and scalability, and can split its switching-speed range between two-dimensional and three-dimensional designs. Would fill mems_2d switching_time, mems_2d crosstalk, mems_2d scaling_limit. Would check mems_3d switching_time.
4. W2089755139, "Digital MEMS for optical switching" (2002). An overview of two-dimensional MEMS switch technology, performance and reliability that should state the loss, speed and crosstalk figures its abstract leaves out. Would fill mems_2d switching_time, mems_2d insertion_loss, mems_2d polarization_dependent_loss, mems_2d crosstalk, mems_2d wavelength_range, mems_2d cost_per_port, mems_2d scaling_limit.
5. W2002555923, "A Multiport Microsecond Optical Circuit Switch for Data Center Networking" (2013). The Mordia system paper, which should say whether its wavelength selective switch is LCoS or another mechanism, and so whether its switching time belongs in the lcos row. Would fill lcos wavelength_range. Would check lcos switching_time.
6. W3034334810, "Principle, Design, and Prototyping of Core Selective Switch Using Free-Space Optics for Spatial Channel Network" (2020). The fullest LCoS device paper in the core set, likely to report crosstalk, polarization dependent loss and the operating band of its prototype. Would fill lcos polarization_dependent_loss, lcos crosstalk, lcos wavelength_range. Would check lcos insertion_loss.
7. W2913395450, "Design and Analysis of Beam Steering Multicore Fiber Optical Switches" (2019). The design paper for piezo beam-steering switches with multicore fibers, the likeliest source of loss budgets, polarization behavior and cost drivers for the piezo row. Would fill piezo polarization_dependent_loss, piezo cost_per_port. Would check piezo port_count, piezo crosstalk, piezo wavelength_range.
8. W2896156020, "Silicon Photonic Switch Fabrics: Technology and Architecture" (2018). A review of silicon photonic switch fabrics across thermo-optic, electro-optic and MEMS actuation, which compares the metrics that the thermo-optic and electro-optic abstracts leave out. Would fill thermo_optic cost_per_port, electro_optic polarization_dependent_loss, electro_optic cost_per_port.
9. W2909208749, "State of the Art and Perspectives on Silicon Photonic Switches" (2019). A review of silicon photonic switches, including MEMS-actuated couplers, that evaluates loss, crosstalk, switching time and packaging and discusses the path from lab to commercialization. Would fill mems_silicon_photonic cost_per_port.
10. W2056973550, "Scalable optical switches for computing applications [Invited]" (2009). A review of SOA-based broadcast-and-select fabrics for computing systems, the likeliest source of wavelength range, polarization and cost figures for the soa row. Would fill soa polarization_dependent_loss, soa wavelength_range, soa cost_per_port. Would check soa port_count.
