# Reading list

Ten core papers whose full text would fill the most matrix cells that abstracts left empty, or settle the widest ranges. Picked by the stage 6 analyst. Title and year come from data/db/papers.sqlite, and the cell lists come from deliverables/comparison_matrix.csv. Written by pipeline.matrix_render.

MEMS is micro-electro-mechanical systems, LCoS is liquid crystal on silicon, and SOA is semiconductor optical amplifier.

1. W2073240697, "Fiber-handling robot and optical connection mechanisms for automatic cross-connection of multiple optical connectors" (2013). The only paper on robotic patch panels, so its full text could fill most of that route. Would fill robotic_patch_panel switching_time, robotic_patch_panel insertion_loss, robotic_patch_panel port_count, robotic_patch_panel polarization_dependent_loss, robotic_patch_panel crosstalk, robotic_patch_panel wavelength_range, robotic_patch_panel cost_per_port, robotic_patch_panel scaling_limit. Would check robotic_patch_panel trl_band.
2. W2914521323, "A Comparative Review of MEMS-Based Optical Cross-Connects for All-Optical Networks From the Past to the Present Day" (2019). A survey of MEMS crossconnects that discusses speed, loss and crosstalk by actuator type, which could fill the 2D MEMS gaps. Would fill mems_2d switching_time, mems_2d polarization_dependent_loss, mems_2d crosstalk.
3. W3034334810, "Principle, Design, and Prototyping of Core Selective Switch Using Free-Space Optics for Spatial Channel Network" (2020). The main LCoS switch prototype. Its full text likely gives crosstalk, polarization loss and band. Would fill lcos polarization_dependent_loss, lcos crosstalk, lcos wavelength_range. Would check lcos insertion_loss.
4. W4292950821, "Mission Apollo: Landing Optical Circuit Switching at Datacenter Scale" (2022). The Apollo production system paper from Google. It would give production figures for 3D MEMS and settle the crosstalk gap. Would fill mems_3d crosstalk.
5. W2909208749, "State of the Art and Perspectives on Silicon Photonic Switches" (2019). A review of silicon photonic switches that evaluates loss, crosstalk and switching time and discusses the path to commercialization. Would fill mems_silicon_photonic cost_per_port, electro_optic cost_per_port.
6. W2896156020, "Silicon Photonic Switch Fabrics: Technology and Architecture" (2018). A review of silicon photonic switch fabrics that compares thermo-optic, electro-optic and MEMS switches on common metrics. Would fill electro_optic polarization_dependent_loss.
7. W1989769976, "Monolithic Nanosecond-Reconfigurable 4$\,\times\,$4 Space and Wavelength Selective Cross-Connect" (2012). A monolithic SOA cross-connect whose full text resolves power loss inside the circuit. Would fill soa insertion_loss, soa wavelength_range. Would check soa port_count.
8. W2089755139, "Digital MEMS for optical switching" (2002). A 2D MEMS technology overview that claims low cost and discusses performance and reliability. Would fill mems_2d wavelength_range, mems_2d cost_per_port.
9. W3215039088, "Core Selective Switch With Low Insertion Loss Over Ultra-Wide Wavelength Range for Spatial Channel Networks" (2021). A 3D MEMS core selective switch whose abstract prints a garbled polarization loss figure. Would fill mems_3d polarization_dependent_loss. Would check mems_3d insertion_loss, mems_3d wavelength_range.
10. W2040047675, "256 $,times,$ 256 Port Optical Cross-Connect Subsystem" (2004). A 3D MEMS crossconnect subsystem paper that compares measured static and dynamic crosstalk. Would fill mems_3d crosstalk. Would check mems_3d insertion_loss.
