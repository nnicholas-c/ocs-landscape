# Reading list

Ten core papers whose full text would fill the most matrix cells that abstracts left empty, or settle the widest ranges. Picked by the stage 6 analyst. Title and year come from data/db/papers.sqlite, and the cell lists come from deliverables/comparison_matrix.csv. Written by pipeline.matrix_render.

MEMS is micro-electro-mechanical systems, LCoS is liquid crystal on silicon, and SOA is semiconductor optical amplifier.

1. W2073240697, "Fiber-handling robot and optical connection mechanisms for automatic cross-connection of multiple optical connectors" (2013). The only core paper on robotic patch panels, so its full text is the one source for nearly every empty cell of that route. Would fill robotic_patch_panel switching_time, robotic_patch_panel insertion_loss, robotic_patch_panel port_count, robotic_patch_panel polarization_dependent_loss, robotic_patch_panel crosstalk, robotic_patch_panel wavelength_range, robotic_patch_panel cost_per_port, robotic_patch_panel scaling_limit. Would check robotic_patch_panel trl_band.
2. W2914521323, "A Comparative Review of MEMS-Based Optical Cross-Connects for All-Optical Networks From the Past to the Present Day" (2019). A review of 2D and 3D MEMS cross-connects that discusses loss, crosstalk, switching speed and scalability, which the 2D MEMS abstracts leave empty. Would fill mems_3d crosstalk, mems_2d switching_time, mems_2d insertion_loss, mems_2d crosstalk, mems_2d scaling_limit.
3. W2896156020, "Silicon Photonic Switch Fabrics: Technology and Architecture" (2018). A survey of thermo-optic, electro-optic and MEMS silicon photonic switch fabrics that compares common metrics, so one read can cover cost and PDL (polarization dependent loss) across three integrated routes. Would fill mems_silicon_photonic cost_per_port, thermo_optic cost_per_port, electro_optic polarization_dependent_loss, electro_optic cost_per_port.
4. W2089755139, "Digital MEMS for optical switching" (2002). The main 2D MEMS switch paper, covering technology, performance and reliability, for the 2D MEMS cells no other abstract touches. Would fill mems_2d polarization_dependent_loss, mems_2d wavelength_range, mems_2d cost_per_port.
5. W3034334810, "Principle, Design, and Prototyping of Core Selective Switch Using Free-Space Optics for Spatial Channel Network" (2020). The LCoS core selective switch prototype, the route's only device paper that names LCoS, for its optical specifications. Would fill lcos polarization_dependent_loss, lcos crosstalk, lcos wavelength_range. Would check lcos insertion_loss.
6. W2591729902, "Beam-Steering All-Optical Switch for Multi-Core Fibers" (2017). The built multi-core fiber beam-steering switch, for the piezo optical specifications and to confirm whether its actuators are piezoelectric. Would fill piezo polarization_dependent_loss, piezo wavelength_range, piezo cost_per_port. Would check piezo port_count, piezo trl_band.
7. W2028213396, "Monolithically Integrated 8:1 SOA Gate Switch With Large Extinction Ratio and Wide Input Power Dynamic Range" (2009). The SOA gate switch paper, for crosstalk and polarization figures behind its extinction ratio and polarization insensitivity claims. Would fill soa polarization_dependent_loss, soa crosstalk. Would check soa insertion_loss.
8. W2056973550, "Scalable optical switches for computing applications [Invited]" (2009). An invited SOA switch fabric study for computing, for the wavelength range and any cost figure of SOA fabrics. Would fill soa wavelength_range, soa cost_per_port.
9. W2002555923, "A Multiport Microsecond Optical Circuit Switch for Data Center Networking" (2013). Mordia, the route's microsecond switching figure, to confirm whether its wavelength-selective switch is LCoS and to find what limits scale. Would fill lcos cost_per_port, lcos scaling_limit. Would check lcos switching_time, lcos ai_cluster_fit.
10. W3215039088, "Core Selective Switch With Low Insertion Loss Over Ultra-Wide Wavelength Range for Spatial Channel Networks" (2021). The MEMS mirror core selective switch whose abstract prints its PDL figure as a garbled LaTeX fragment, so only the full text can give the 3D MEMS PDL value. Would fill mems_3d polarization_dependent_loss. Would check mems_3d insertion_loss, mems_3d wavelength_range.
